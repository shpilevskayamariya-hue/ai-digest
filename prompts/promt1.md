#context
You are writing for a motherhood-focused blog about emotional wellbeing and life balance after childbirth.
The article should support mothers in the first three months with a newborn, helping them feel calmer, less alone, and more grounded.
The tone must be warm, human, and reassuring — not clinical, not alarming.
The content should normalize struggles and avoid pressure or “perfect parenting” expectations.

#references
Use general psychological and public health research on postpartum mental health (e.g. WHO, APA, peer-reviewed studies).
Do not cite specific papers in-text, but base statements on widely accepted knowledge and statistics.

#task
Write an article explaining:
- what postpartum depression is (in simple, non-clinical language)
- how it may feel emotionally and mentally in early motherhood
- gentle, practical ways a woman can support herself in the first 3 months
- how to recognize signs that extra support from a specialist may be helpful
- include global statistics to normalize the experience

#format
- Headline: max 80 characters
- Length: ~500 words
- Structure:
  - short introduction
  - 3 subtitles (clear and supportive)
  - conclusion with gentle encouragement
- Add a short reference section at the end (e.g. WHO, APA, etc.)

#constraints
- Use only factual, research-based information
- Do not diagnose or label the reader
- Do not recommend medication or treatment plans
- Do not blame, shame, or compare mothers
- Avoid fear-based or alarming language
- Keep wording simple and emotionally supportive
- Offer suggestions, not strict instructions
- Include gentle disclaimers where appropriate (e.g. suggesting consulting a specialist if needed)
