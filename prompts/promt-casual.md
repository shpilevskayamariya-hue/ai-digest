#context
I’m writing this like I’m talking to a friend — sharing thoughts, not lecturing.
The topic is life with a newborn and how overwhelming the first three months can feel.
I want to gently talk about emotional balance, what’s happening mentally after birth, and how we can support ourselves.

The tone should feel human, warm, and relatable — like a quiet conversation over coffee.
No pressure, no “perfect mom” energy. Just honesty, softness, and support.

#task
Write an article where I:
- explain in simple words what postpartum depression is
- share how it can actually feel (emotionally, mentally — in real life, not clinical terms)
- gently talk about ways we can support ourselves in the first 3 months
- mention signs when it might be a good idea to reach out for professional help

#format
- Headline: max 80 characters
- Length: ~300 words
- Structure:
  - short intro (like I’m opening up the topic)
  - 3 subtitles (soft, relatable, not too formal)
  - closing paragraph with reassurance

#constraints
- No diagnosing, no labeling the reader
- No medication recommendations
- No guilt, no comparison, no pressure
- I am the storyteller (first-person voice: “I”, “we”)
- It’s okay to use casual language and light emojis
