## HIGH Priority — write first

### 1. Postpartum Depression Diagnoses Nearly Doubled
**Priority:** HIGH
**Why:** Directly relevant to mothers in the postpartum period — core audience topic.
**Angle:** Not alarming, but normalizing: "you're not alone — it's more visible because talking about it has become normal."
**Key facts:**
- Diagnoses rose from 9.4% (2010) to 19% (2021)
- Mental health is now the #1 cause of maternal death
- Only 26% of new mothers receive any education about PPD
**Disclaimer:** required (maternal health)
**Tags:** `postpartum`, `mental health`, `motherhood`

---

### 2. Mothers' Mental Health Reports Drop 12 Points
**Priority:** HIGH
**Why:** Emotional wellbeing of mothers — directly on-topic for the digest.
**Angle:** Validate mothers' exhaustion + gentle practical steps. Not a statistics horror story.
**Key facts:**
- "Excellent" mental health reports: from 38.4% (2016) to 25.8% (2023)
- Fair/poor mental health reports increased by 64%
- Drivers: economic stress, lack of support, racial inequality
**Disclaimer:** required
**Tags:** `mental health`, `motherhood`, `wellbeing`

---

### 3. New US Dietary Guidelines for Children Released
**Priority:** HIGH
**Why:** Nutrition is a core topic for mothers with 0–2 year olds — directly in the audience's area of interest.
**Angle:** What changed for infants and toddlers, explained in plain language.
**Key facts:**
- First guidelines with age-specific recommendations: from infants through adolescents
- Focus on whole, minimally processed foods
- Issued by HHS + USDA (official source)
**Disclaimer:** required (child health)
**Tags:** `nutrition`, `feeding`, `baby`, `guidelines`

---

### 4. Paid Family Leave Expands in US States
**Priority:** HIGH
**Why:** Practically useful for new mothers; postpartum period is a core topic.
**Angle:** What specifically changed and how it helps mothers with newborns.
**Key facts:**
- New programs launched: Delaware, Maine, Maryland
- Colorado became the first state to offer paid NICU leave (12 extra weeks)
- Mississippi, Tennessee expanded parental leave for public sector workers
**Disclaimer:** not required
**Tags:** `parental leave`, `postpartum`, `support`

---

### 5. Gen Z Parents Moving Away from Extreme "Gentle Parenting"
**Priority:** HIGH
**Why:** A timely cultural shift in parenting; resonates strongly with young mothers.
**Angle:** Not judgment, but relief — "saying no is okay."
**Key facts:**
- Shift from "never say no" to a hybrid style with clear boundaries
- Pediatricians and psychologists welcome the change
- Trend among Gen Z parents (born ~1997–2012)
**Disclaimer:** not required
**Tags:** `parenting`, `gentle parenting`, `child development`

## MEDIUM Priority — write after high

### 6. Families Embrace "Analog Living" Anti-Smartphone Movement
**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Key facts:**

- Board games, less screen time, less over-scheduling
- Parents are consciously choosing a "slower" childhood
**Disclaimer:** not required
**Tags:** `screen time`, `parenting`, `analog`, `wellbeing`

### 7. Global Childhood Obesity to Hit 228 Million by 2040
**Priority:** MEDIUM
**Why:** Child nutrition is an audience topic, but the headline is fear-based — requires careful framing.
**Angle:** Early eating habits — what parents can do right now, without fear-mongering.
**Key facts:**
- 188 million children already affected today
- For the first time, obesity surpasses undernutrition in school-age children
- Driver: ultra-processed foods replacing whole foods

**Tags:** `nutrition`, `child health`, `feeding`
