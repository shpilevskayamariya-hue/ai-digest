# e-lactancia.org — Summary

**URL:** https://www.e-lactancia.org/
**Extracted:** April 16, 2026

## What it is

A free Spanish-language database run by APILAM (Asociación para la Promoción e Investigación científica y cultural de la Lactancia Materna). It lets breastfeeding mothers and healthcare professionals quickly check whether a medication, herb, supplement, cosmetic, or food is safe to use while nursing.

## Key facts

- Covers **40,691+ terms** — drugs, herbal remedies, homeopathy, contaminants, medical procedures, cosmetics
- Maintained by **pediatricians and pharmacists**
- Last updated: **April 11, 2026**
- Recommended by the **Academy of Breastfeeding Medicine** (USA, 2012)
- Free; runs on donations (€1/month via Teaming)

## Founder

Dr. José María Paricio Talayero — pediatrician, founder & president of APILAM.

## Notable positions

- Breastfeeding is the healthiest option for babies and mothers in all circumstances; very few situations justify contraindicating it.
- Opposes war explicitly — cites that conflict reduces breastfeeding prevalence and increases formula use, posing serious risks to infant health.
- Supports WHO 2004 Principle 3: strict control of breast milk substitutes in emergency settings.
