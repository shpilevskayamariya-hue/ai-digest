# Motherhood & Child News — 15 Stories (2025–2026)

1. **Black Maternal Mortality Gap Widens** — Black women die at 3.5x the rate of white women; the disparity grew despite overall US maternal deaths declining. *(Feb 2025)*

2. **US Senate Advances Maternal Health Funding for 2026** — $3M for CDC Safe Motherhood + $20M for NIH maternal death research. *(Aug 2025)*

3. **Global Childhood Obesity to Hit 228 Million by 2040** — Obesity now surpasses undernutrition in school-age children for the first time ever. *(Mar 2026)*

4. **Countries Banning Social Media for Children** — Denmark, UK, Spain, Malaysia and US states (MA, VA) moving to restrict under-14/16 access. *(Apr 2026)*

5. **Postpartum Depression Diagnoses Nearly Doubled** — From 9.4% in 2010 to 19% in 2021; mental health now the #1 cause of maternal death. *(2025)*

6. **Children's Mental Health Still a National Emergency** — 40%+ of US high schoolers report persistent sadness; 60% of depressed teens get no treatment. *(Oct 2025)*

7. **Families Embrace "Analog Living" Anti-Smartphone Movement** — Board games, no-phone childhoods, and less scheduling becoming a mainstream parenting trend. *(2026)*

8. **New US Dietary Guidelines for Children Released** — First guidelines with age-specific recommendations from infancy through adolescence, emphasizing whole foods. *(Late 2025)*

9. **Paid Family Leave Expands in US States** — Delaware, Maine, Maryland launched programs; Colorado became first state to offer paid NICU leave. *(Jan 2026)*

10. **Mothers' "Excellent" Mental Health Reports Drop 12 Points** — Down from 38.4% to 25.8% since 2016; fair/poor mental health reports up 64%. *(2025)*

11. **WHO Urges Schools to Fix Children's Nutrition** — New global guidance targets ultra-processed foods harming kids' cognitive development and mental health. *(Jan 2026)*

12. **UK Schools Get Sweeping Safeguarding Reforms** — Mandatory AI safety guidance, default phone-free policy, 24 proposed changes to school safety rules. *(Feb 2026)*

13. **Gen Z Parents Moving Away from Extreme "Gentle Parenting"** — Shift toward hybrid styles with clear rules; pediatricians welcome the change. *(2026)*

14. **US Senate Prioritizes Maternal Mental Health in 2026 Budget** — 96% of the US birth-giving population lives in areas with too few perinatal mental health specialists. *(2025)*

15. **Australia (NSW) Launches Child Safety Reforms** — New oversight regulations for childcare and early education settings. *(Apr 2026)*
